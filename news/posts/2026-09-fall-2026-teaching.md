The Fall 2026 term at the **University of the Fraser Valley** is under way. This semester I am teaching five sections:

| Course | Title | Enrolment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| COMP 370 | Software Engineering (two sections) | 25 + 27 |
| COMP 481 | Functional and Logic Programming | 28 |

| CIS 285 | End User Training and Support | 23 |

## Software engineering, with industry in the room

COMP 370 is the course I have taught every term since joining UFV. During my term as Chair of Industry Partnerships in the School of Computing (Fall 2025 to September 2026), I worked to bring industry practice into the classroom. In earlier offerings, guest practitioners from Intuit Mailchimp and Caseway brought real requirements into the classroom.

## By the numbers

Across my completed and current UFV terms, I have taught **nine sections** and **237 course enrolments**. That counts seats rather than unique students, since some students take more than one course with me.
