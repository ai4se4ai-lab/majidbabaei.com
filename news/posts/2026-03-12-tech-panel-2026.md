On **March 12, 2026**, the School of Computing at the University of the Fraser Valley hosted **Tech Panel 2026**, a panel and networking session I organized with the **Computing Student Association**. Colleagues and staff across the school supported it.

## The panel

- **Colin Schmidt**, Zynim
- **Meer Anwar**, PixelPaddle
- **Mason Paquette**, Kwixand
- **Stan Engelbrecht**, ADAMnetworks

## Why events like this matter

As Chair of Industry Partnerships, my goal is to build relationships that support **experiential learning, curriculum relevance, applied research, and professional pathways** for our students. A panel is one of the simplest ways to do that: students hear directly from people building products, and companies meet the students who will soon join them.

Tech Panel 2026 built on the industry events of Fall 2025. These included a cloud-computing talk by Craig Slack, CEO of Invero and a UFV alumnus, and a technical-interview event with Richard Clark of Crafty Penguins and UFV career-education partners.

*Thank you to everyone who made the evening possible.*
