My term as **Chair of Industry Partnerships** in the **School of Computing at the University of the Fraser Valley** ended in **September 2026**. I served in this role from Fall 2025.

The role focused on relationships supporting experiential learning, curriculum relevance, applied research, and professional pathways for computing students. Activities included industry talks, bringing practitioners into the classroom, and [Tech Panel 2026](/news/tech-panel-2026/), organized with the Computing Student Association and support from colleagues and staff.

This update concerns the chair responsibility. I continue as a **tenure-track Assistant Professor** in UFV's School of Computing, with my teaching and research responsibilities.

The [service section](/#service) and [CV](mailto:majid.babaei@ufv.ca?subject=Latest%20CV%20request) now record the completed term. Earlier announcements remain dated accounts of activities during the appointment.
