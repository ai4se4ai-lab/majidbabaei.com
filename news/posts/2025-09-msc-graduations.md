This month, two students I co-advised with **Prof. Naser Ezzati-Jivan** at **Brock University** completed their master's theses. Both started in January 2024.

## Ghazal Khodabandeh

**Thesis:** *Link Prediction on Distributed Systems*

Ghazal's work on graph neural networks for link prediction in microservice architectures appeared at **ICPE 2025**, the ACM/SPEC International Conference on Performance Engineering. She is now a **Research Assistant at York University**.

## Alireza Ezaz

**Thesis:** *TAAF: A Trace Abstraction and Analysis Framework Synergizing Knowledge Graphs and LLMs*

Alireza's thesis became the **TAAF** paper at **ICSE 2026**. It combines time-indexed knowledge graphs with LLMs to help developers question large execution traces. He is now a **Data Quality Specialist at Cohere**.

## Thank you

Congratulations to you both, and thank you for your work on our shared papers.
