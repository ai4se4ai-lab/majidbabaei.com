My updated research profile asks what evidence a computing system should require before acting on an AI-generated result. It connects replay and regression testing, service-trace explanation and prediction, and explicit dependencies between agents' results. The long-term question is which earlier conclusions remain dependable after a change and what additional evidence engineers need before acting.
