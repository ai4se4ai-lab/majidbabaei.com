Two pieces of our work appear at **ICSE 2026**:

- **TAAF**, a research paper on trace abstraction and analysis with knowledge graphs and LLMs, from Alireza Ezaz's M.Sc. thesis at Brock University.
- **SIKG**, a poster on semantic impact analysis for adaptive test selection.

The conference week is recorded here from the public ICSE 2026 programme; the days are marked tentative until travel is settled. Read more in the newsroom: [Two papers at ICSE 2026, TAAF and SIKG](/news/icse-2026-taaf-sikg/).
