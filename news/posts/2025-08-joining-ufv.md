This August I joined the **School of Computing at the University of the Fraser Valley (UFV)** in British Columbia as a **tenure-track Assistant Professor**. I am also taking on the role of **Chair of Industry Partnerships** for the school.

## The road here

- **McGill University, School of Continuing Studies** (2023–2025): Faculty Lecturer, then Assistant Professor (Professional) and Academic Program Coordinator.
- **McGill University, Electrical and Computer Engineering**: Associate Member since November 2024, an affiliation that continues.
- **Concordia University** (2022–2023): Postdoctoral Research Fellow working on software robustness analysis with Prof. Yann-Gaël Guéhéneuc.
- **Queen's University** (Ph.D., 2021): regression testing of distributed real-time embedded systems in model-driven development, supervised by Prof. Juergen Dingel.

Alongside and before my academic positions, I worked as a software developer and DevOps engineer, at Imaginary Spaces, the National Research Council Canada, Robert Bosch GmbH, EPAK GmbH, and Farazpajohan.

## What I will be working on

At UFV I teach software engineering and programming, starting with COMP 370 (Software Engineering) and COMP 481 (Functional and Logic Programming) this fall. I also continue my research at the intersection of **model-driven engineering, software engineering, and AI**, with the [anonymous for review].
