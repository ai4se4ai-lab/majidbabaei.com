Reviewing and organizing are a large part of how a research community works. In 2026 I am serving in three roles:

- **CASCON 2026**: Publicity Co-Chair on the organizing committee ([committee page](https://conf.researchr.org/committee/cascon-2026/cascon-2026-organizing-committee)).
- **ASE 2026**: Program Committee, Tools and Datasets track ([committee page](https://conf.researchr.org/committee/ase-2026/ase-2026-tools-and-data-sets-program-committee)).
- **FSE 2026**: Program Committee, Student Research Competition ([committee page](https://conf.researchr.org/committee/fse-2026/fse-2026-student-research-competition-program-committee)).

These build on my 2025 roles as Proceedings Co-Chair for ICSE 2025, Publicity Co-Chair for SEAMS 2025, and Publicity Chair for SERP4IoT 2025. I also served on program committees for ASE (tool demonstrations), MODELS (workshops), and ICPE (data challenge).
